About

One firm. Every discipline. Built to deliver.

Building Measurements Services is a Canadian multidisciplinary firm uniting architecture, engineering, design and advisory under one platform, with the practical construction knowledge to keep solutions buildable, efficient and viable.

Who we are

We're not a small studio or a drafting shop. We're an integrated professional services firm for construction and real estate development, capable of sitting across the table from developers, institutions and general contractors on complex, large-scale projects.

The short version

We coordinate multiple technical disciplines through a single point of contact, so owners and contractors get design that is not only compelling architecturally, but also constructible, efficient and economically sound.

Rooted in Canadian professional standards, with the capacity to deliver internationally, partnering with licensed professionals in each jurisdiction where required.
